Pusu: Uyanış original artwork

The artwork in this directory is derived only from the original Pusu media
identified by the source paths and SHA-256 checksums in provenance.json.
The project user explicitly approved use of original game artwork for the
native installer and launcher. Original artwork remains subject to its
original rights holders' rights; this notice does not claim a new public
licence or grant rights to distribute the rest of the game.

The AppImage includes only the named installer illustration and application
icon, not the original game's asset library. Other assets must be imported
from the user's own original disc image. The native implementation's authorship
and third-party library licence/source notices are recorded separately.
